Kinetic-type storyboard and animatic · 2026-10-03 12:13 · motion-designer

No Adobe app was used (this run doesn't allow screen control). I wrote the spec as Markdown and rendered the preview with ffmpeg 9.0.2 `drawtext`.

**Storyboard ("MAKE IT MOVE"):** 1920x1080, 30 fps, 180 frames (0–179). Five beats:
1. **Build, frames 0–35:** the three words come in 12 frames apart. "MAKE" rises, "IT" slides in from the right, "MOVE" drops from above in the accent colour.
2. **Hold, frames 36–89.**
3. **Exit, frames 90–101:** all three words fade out together.
4. **"6 SECONDS.", frames 102–161:** in, hold, then out.
5. **End card, frames 162–179.**

**Easing:** entrances use ease-out cubic, the "IT" slide uses ease-out quint, and exits use ease-in cubic. The spec gives each curve's formula and an approximate cubic-bezier value for the After Effects build. The timing table lists, for each property, the in/out frame, duration, from→to values and easing.

**Preview:** every keyframe is driven by the frame index, so the timing is frame-exact. Frame number, timecode and beat label are burned in along the bottom of the frame.

**ffprobe:** h264, 1920x1080, yuv420p, 30/1. Duration is 6.000000 s for both the container and the stream, and 180 frames were counted. I checked a contact sheet of frames 6, 18, 30, 60, 96, 120, 165 and 179 against the timing table, and they match.

The first render failed because ffmpeg 9 no longer has `-filter_complex_script`. `render.sh` now uses `-/filter_complex <file>`.
