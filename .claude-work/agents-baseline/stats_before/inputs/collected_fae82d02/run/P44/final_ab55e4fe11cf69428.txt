RESULT: The pricing section is built in Tailwind v4.3.3 with three plans (Starter, Pro, Team), a Monthly/Annual switch and colour tokens for light and dark mode. Screenshots exist at 360, 768 and 1280 px, each in light and dark (6 total).
- **Tokens:** semantic colours are CSS variables on `:root`, written in OKLCH. The page follows the system's light or dark setting; adding `data-theme="light"` or `data-theme="dark"` to `<html>` forces one. Tailwind reads the variables through `@theme inline`, so classes like `bg-surface` and `text-fg-muted` switch theme on their own and the markup has no `dark:` classes.
- **Type scale:** steps `text-step--1` to `text-step-5`, each a `clamp()` between 360 and 1280 px. Body text grows from 16 to 18 px, and the scale ratio from 1.2 to 1.25. Section padding scales with the viewport the same way.
- **Layout:** one column on phones, two at 768 px (Team then fills the full width below), three from 1024 px up.
