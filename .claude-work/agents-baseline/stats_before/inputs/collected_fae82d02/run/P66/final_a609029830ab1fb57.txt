- **Size and bleed:** the page is 6.125 × 9.25 in. That is the 6 × 9 in trim plus 0.125 in bleed on the outside, top and bottom edges, and none on the spine edge (the KDP/IngramSpark convention).
- **Mirrored margins:** set from the trim line with Typst's `inside`/`outside` margins and `binding: left`. Inside is 0.75 in (the 0.5 in minimum gutter for 151–300 pages, plus some room), outside 0.5 in, top 0.75 in, bottom 0.75 in. The outside, top and bottom values have the bleed added on the page.
- **Running heads:** left-hand pages show the folio and the book title; right-hand pages show the current chapter and the folio. Front matter and chapter-opening pages have no running head, and chapters always start on a right-hand page. `main-matter()` restarts page numbers at 1 on a right-hand page.
- **Spine and cover formula:** spine = pages ÷ PPI × 1 in (PPI = pages per inch). The full cover spread is 2 × 6 + spine + 0.25 in wide and 9.25 in tall. With 220 pages and the placeholder 444 PPI, the spine is 0.4955 in and the cover is 12.7455 × 9.25 in. The copyright page prints these computed values.
- **Proof marks:** setting `show-trim = true` draws the trim box and the text-area box on each page for proofing.
- **Pandoc route:** `build.sh` notes how to convert Markdown chapters to Typst with `pandoc -t typst`.
