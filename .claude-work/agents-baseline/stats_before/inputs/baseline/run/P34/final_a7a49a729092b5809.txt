**Pipeline** (`Pipeline.kt`): `Flow<T>.debounceRetry(window, policy, transform)` chains `debounce(window)` into `mapLatest`, and each call goes through `retryWithBackoff`.
- A new value after the window cancels the in-flight call and its backoff sleeps.
- Each retry waits `min(initialDelay * factor^n, maxDelay)`.
- Cancellation is never retried, and a `retryOn` predicate decides which errors are retried.
- Failures that exhaust the attempts come out as `Result.failure`, so one bad input doesn't end the flow.

**Tests** (`PipelineTest.kt`, all on `runTest` virtual time):
- **Debounce burst:** emissions at t=0, 50 and 100 produce nothing at t=199 and only `c` at t=200.
- **Debounce spacing:** values spaced exactly one window apart both emit, at t=100 and t=200.
- **Backoff timing:** attempts happen at t=100, 200, 400 and 800, so the gaps are 100, 200 and 400 ms. The result arrives at t=800.
- **Delay cap:** with `maxDelay` of 250 ms the gaps are 100, 200, 250, 250 and 250.
- **Exhaustion:** four attempts, a final failure, and total virtual time of 700 ms.
- **Non-retryable error:** fails after one attempt with no virtual time spent.
- **Cancellation:** a new value during backoff cancels the old retry chain. Old attempts run at t=100 and 200, and the third (t=400) never happens; `new` runs at t=250.
