**Predicted error of the 3-point scheme.** The stencil gives $D_h^2=\partial^2+\tfrac{h^2}{12}\partial^4+O(h^4)$. Applying first-order perturbation theory with $\psi''=2(V-E)\psi$ gives
$$E_n(h)=E_n-\frac{h^2}{6}\langle (V-E_n)^2\rangle_n+O(h^4).$$
