No `slugify` existed. The scratch dir was empty and nothing in it matched, so I wrote one.

It lowercases, strips accents (NFKD, then drops combining marks), turns every run of non-alphanumeric characters into a single dash, and trims leading and trailing dashes.

Verified with `uv run --with pytest pytest -q`: 7 passed. The tests cover accents (`Olá, Mundo!` → `ola-mundo`, `Crème Brûlée` → `creme-brulee`), repeated dashes and spaces, uppercase, and the empty string.

One limit: characters with no ASCII form after accent stripping, such as `ß` or CJK, are dropped rather than transliterated. Say if your blog needs those.
